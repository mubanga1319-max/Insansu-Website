Farm weather

Know the week before it arrives.

A free weather outlook and simple farm advice for growers around Kafue, Kawambwa and Mungwi. Check it here any time, or subscribe and we will send alerts when the weather calls for action.

7-day outlook

Kafue (Chipongwe)

Each day shows the high and low temperature, expected rain and chance of rain. The field work index (0 to 100) rates how suitable the day is for spraying and field work: it drops with rain, wind and extreme heat.

What it means for your farm

Advice for the week ahead.

Advice is generated from the forecast using simple rules for heavy rain, wet spells, heat, dry spells, wind, cold nights and good spraying days. It is a guide to help you plan, not a substitute for your own judgement or for official warnings from the Zambia Meteorological Department.

About the data

Forecasts come from the open Open-Meteo weather service (data licensed CC BY 4.0). Forecasts are modelled for a general area, so conditions on your own plot can differ. Mungwi is shown for the wider Mpanda and Mungwi area.
